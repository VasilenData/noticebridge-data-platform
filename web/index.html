01 / SOURCE

Public records, bounded coverage.

Notices come from the official TED source. The capture time and selection are shown here. This is not a complete inventory of available contracts.

02 / COMPARISON

Linked versions, explicit uncertainty.

A comparison requires an identified parent in the captured data. Missing or ambiguous parents stay unresolved. Similar titles alone never establish a revision.

03 / MATCHING

Rules you can inspect.

Buyer country, procurement code and keyword signals help explain relevance. A buyer’s country is not necessarily the place of delivery. Rules do not establish eligibility or predict an award.
